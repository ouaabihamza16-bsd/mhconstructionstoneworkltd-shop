About us

A small building firm that also does the stone

MH CONSTRUCTION & STONEWORK LTD is a private limited company based in Edgware, Middlesex. It was incorporated on 9 October 2019 and is registered in England and Wales under company number 12253041.

Our work is on houses. We take on domestic building work such as extensions, renovations and structural alterations, alongside stonework and masonry, stone patios and garden walls, brickwork and general repairs.

Because the same small team covers both the building and the stone or brick finish, you deal with one firm for the whole job. The company is owner-managed, and our clients are homeowners and private landlords.

Services

What we do

Four areas of work, all on homes. Many jobs touch more than one of them, which is where having one team for the building and the stonework helps.

Home Building & Extensions

Building work on houses, from adding space with an extension to renovating rooms or making structural alterations to the existing building.

  • House extensions
  • Renovations
  • Structural alterations

Stonework & Masonry

Work in natural and reconstituted stone, whether that is building a new stone wall, cladding a wall in stone, adding a stone feature or repointing existing stonework.

  • Stone walls
  • Stone cladding
  • Stone features
  • Repointing

Patios & Hard Landscaping

Stone for the outside of the house: patios, paths, steps and garden walls, laid as part of a larger job or as a project in its own right.

  • Stone patios
  • Paths
  • Steps
  • Garden walls

Repairs & Brickwork

Work on existing homes that need putting right: brickwork, repointing tired or damaged mortar joints, and general building repairs.

  • Brickwork
  • Repointing
  • General building repairs
